# Cardiovascular diseases (CVDs)

## Key facts

- CVDs represent the top cause of mortality worldwide.
- Approximately 19.8 million deaths occurred from CVDs in 2022, accounting for roughly 32% of total global fatalities.
- Heart attacks and strokes were responsible for 85% of these deaths.
- Over 75% of CVD deaths happen in low- and middle-income nations.
- Of the 18 million premature deaths (under age 70) from noncommunicable diseases in 2021, CVDs caused at least 38%.
- Most CVDs are preventable through lifestyle modifications and environmental improvements.
- Early detection enables better disease management through counseling and pharmaceutical interventions.

## Overview

CVDs comprise multiple cardiac and vascular disorders:

- Coronary heart disease (blood vessel disease affecting the heart)
- Cerebrovascular disease (blood vessel disease affecting the brain)
- Peripheral arterial disease (affecting limb blood vessels)
- Rheumatic heart disease (valve and muscle damage from rheumatic fever)
- Congenital heart disease (birth-related structural heart defects)
- Deep vein thrombosis and pulmonary embolism (blood clots)

Heart attacks and strokes typically result from blockages preventing blood flow, usually caused by fatty deposit accumulation on vessel walls. Strokes may also involve vessel rupture or clot formation.

## Risk factors for cardiovascular disease

**Behavioral factors:**
- Unhealthy diet (excess salt, sugar, fats)
- Physical inactivity
- Tobacco use
- Harmful alcohol consumption

**Environmental factors:**
- Air pollution

**Intermediate risk factors** (measurable in primary care):
- Elevated blood pressure
- Elevated blood glucose
- Elevated blood lipids
- Overweight and obesity

**Underlying determinants:**
- Poverty
- Stress
- Hereditary factors
- Globalization and urbanization
- Population aging

## Common symptoms of cardiovascular diseases

### Heart attack symptoms

- Chest pain or discomfort
- Arm, shoulder, elbow, jaw, or back pain
- Breathing difficulty or shortness of breath
- Nausea or vomiting
- Light-headedness or fainting
- Cold sweats
- Pale appearance

Women may experience atypical symptoms including shortness of breath, nausea, vomiting, and back or jaw pain.

### Stroke symptoms

- Sudden facial, arm, or leg weakness (often one-sided)
- Numbness of face, arm, or leg
- Confusion or speech difficulties
- Vision problems
- Walking difficulty, dizziness, or loss of balance
- Severe unexplained headache
- Fainting or unconsciousness

**Seek immediate medical care** if experiencing these symptoms.

## Reducing the cardiovascular disease burden

**Prevention strategies:**
- Tobacco cessation
- Dietary salt reduction
- Increased fruit and vegetable consumption
- Regular physical activity
- Limiting harmful alcohol use
- Air quality improvement

**Health system integration:**
- Including CVD management in universal health coverage
- Implementing primary care hypertension programs (demonstrated as efficient and cost-effective)

**Essential medications:**
- Aspirin
- Beta-blockers
- Calcium channel blockers
- Angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitors
- Diuretics
- Statins

**Surgical interventions:**
- Coronary artery bypass
- Balloon angioplasty
- Valve repair and replacement
- Heart transplantation
- Artificial heart operations
